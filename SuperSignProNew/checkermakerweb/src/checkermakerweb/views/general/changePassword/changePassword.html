Change Password

  1. Home
  2. Change Password


  1. Enter your old login password in the Old Login Password field.
  2. Enter your new login password in the New Login Password field. This shall be the password that you want to change.
  3. Reconfirm your new login password by entering the same new login password value in the Confirm Login Password field.
  4. Click on UPDATE button to change your login password.
  5. Never share your password with anyone.
  6. Always create strong and complex passwords.
  7. We recommend you to use mix patterns like One Capital, One Small, Numbers and Special Characters while creating your passwords.
